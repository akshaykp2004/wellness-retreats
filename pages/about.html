12 Private Forest Cabins

Hand-built from reclaimed cedar. Each cabin overlooks a private forest clearing with a meditation deck.

🧘
Main Meditation Hall

2,000 sq ft of silent space. Earthen floor, natural light, surrounded by ancient oak trees on all sides.

🌊
Natural Stone Pool

Fed by a natural spring. Mineral-rich water, no chemicals. Open from 6 AM – 9 PM daily.

🍽️
Farm-to-Table Dining Hall

Open-air, fire-lit. All produce grown on our 5-acre organic farm. Zero processed food. Ever.

Herbal Medicine Garden

Over 80 medicinal plants. Guests are invited to harvest, learn, and prepare their own herbal teas.

💆
Healing Arts Center

4 private treatment rooms. Massage, Ayurvedic therapies, sound healing, and counseling sessions.

Forest Trails

Three immersive walking paths through our ancient forest sanctuary.

🌲
The Ancestor Path
2.5 km · Easy · 45 min

A gentle loop through the oldest trees. Silent walking practice. Dawn and dusk magic.

🌾
The Meadow Walk
1.8 km · Easy · 30 min

Opens to a wildflower meadow with views of the valley. Perfect for morning metta practice.

⛰️
The Summit Trail
4.2 km · Moderate · 90 min

Ascends to a rocky outlook with a 360° forest panorama. Sunrise hike optional every 3rd day.
